Authors: Jiahui Yu, Zhe Lin, Jimei Yang, Xiaohui Shen, Xin Lu, Thomas Huang.

The source repository publishes these files as DeepFill image inpainting examples, associated with Contextual Attention (CVPR 2018) and Gated Convolution (ICCV 2019). These PNG files are copied byte-for-byte. The page only scales images and clips layers for side-by-side comparison. No pixels were retouched, regenerated, or replaced. Per-file sources and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in manifest.json.

These are published results from the foundational method, NOT a local execution or performance evaluation of zuruoke/watermark-removal. Only case1 includes a visible text-watermark removal example (along with rail removal); the other cases show object/person removal with supplied masks. They do not establish automatic watermark detection capability.
